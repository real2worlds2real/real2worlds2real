# Object review

Review the model's structure before repeated numerical fitting. A source-based
rejection of the representation must send the agent back to construction;
parameter tuning cannot turn the wrong component layout or fold pattern into
the observed object. An optional `fit.representation_review` names a
config-relative JSON with `version: 1`, `object`, `model_sha256`, `basis`,
`decision` (`refine_parameters` or `replace_representation`), and nonempty
`source_evidence` / `candidate_evidence` maps of session-relative files to SHA256.
The fit and export commands reject a failed or stale review. A permitted fit
still needs all existing geometry, semantic and physical checks. This control
applies equally to rigid and flexible models and does not relax their limits.

Every object and every table / counter top of a built scene, the twin and each cousin, is checked automatically,
rendered close up beside the photo and judged by the agent the way a photograph would be judged: does it stand on
something, at its real size, in its real material, complete, on a surface that looks like the material it is. What
fails goes into the scene graph as a fix that the scene builder applies in the twin and all cousins alike.

```
configs/<name>/scene_graph.json  (fixes, see below)
  -> calibrated_scene_reconstruction.recon.setpieces sessions/<name>            objects/<n>/kit.json   (photo measurements of kit fixes)
  -> apps/review_objects.py sessions/<name> --tag T [--variant cousins/cousin_k.json]
       qa/objects/<T>/{<item>.jpg, sheet_<k>.jpg, surfaces_<k>.jpg, overview.jpg}, qa/objects_<T>.json
  -> apps/object_closeups.py sessions/<name>        (walk frames posed: parsing/posed_frames/cameras.npz)
       qa/object_closeups/<object>.jpg   (each manipulable object beside the walk's nearest frames of it)
  -> the agent: fixes + a top-level `_object_review` note          (pipeline stage object_review; rerun --from clearance)
```

## Automatic checks (`sim.object_review`, `apps/review_objects.py`)

Items: `/World/Static` (reconstructions, templates, library swaps, set pieces), `/World/Support` (movable furniture),
`/World/Synthetic` (furniture the panorama completion invented), `/World/Objects` (the manipulable ones).

| check | how | fails when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| support | PhysX rays straight down from the item's lowest points to the first collider that is not the item | the smallest gap is over 1.2 cm (floating) or the median under -2 cm (sunk); a frame / clock / shelf within 4 cm of a wall and a lamp within 15 cm of the ceiling need none |
| overlap | a ray whose hit distance is 0 started inside that collider | the item intersects another (a stool standing in a table's base) |
| balance | rays down on an 8 x 8 grid over the item's whole footprint, from 3 cm above its base | under 85 % of its footprint over a surface at its base (it overhangs), or the centre of its points outside the supported cells' hull (it would tip); the support rays above start from its lowest points only, which a box overhanging a desk's edge keeps on the desk. Items on the floor or hung are not checked |
| collider | a room object's box collider against its visible points' footprint | the box reaches past what is seen (visible share under 85 %): an invisible ledge things stand on. Library stand-ins are stretched across to the measured box (`sim.scene.fit_footprint`, height kept, 0.5-2.5x per axis) and, when the box is long (1.3:1 or more), turned to the room's nearest axis first. |
| size | height and largest footprint side against the class's range (`SIZES`, 15 % slack) | a 40 cm mug, a 2 m book |
| material | how the item is built | a glass or metal class built as an opaque photo-textured shell |

A plant in a basket or fruit in a bowl intersects its container and rests on what is inside it: such pairs
(`CONTAINER` x `HELD`) are no overlap, and the held item is not floating.

Renders: per item the photo's crop of its instance mask and two close views (from the photo's side and 70 degrees
round it); per table / counter top a view from above at an angle (under the ceiling) beside the photo's crop of it;
an overview from the photo camera. The automatic checks find what geometry shows; the agent finds the rest in the
renders: a projected texture smeared by what stood on the top, a row of books that is five books, glass that renders
as chrome, a cooktop baked into a counter texture with a jagged edge.

A cousin's own `room_overrides` win over furniture looks.

**Kits** (`assets.kits`): `book_row` (books spine-out, spine colours from the photo, in a wooden or painted metal
rack), `tray_set` (a stainless tray, stacking glass tumblers, coffee pots), `cooktop` (black glass in a steel edge,
zone outlines optional), `pedestal_table` (a round wooden top on a black column and cross base), `cylinder` (a
plain peg or dowel, lying or standing, its ends chamfered), `plate_post_hole` (a peg-in-hole fixture: a base plate, a
post with rounded vertical edges, a round hole down it; collision in convex pieces round the hole), `cup` (an open
tapered cup, its wall and foot thick, a rolled lip optional), `decal` (a flat mark of one or more outlines).

**Photo measurement** (`recon.setpieces`): SAM 3 finds the whole thing from the fix's `prompt` (the instance masks
often hold part of a row: 7 of 22 spines), its pixels are lifted with the photo's point map (`parsing/geometry.npz`;
rays onto the support plane are ill-conditioned at a grazing camera angle: 6.4 m for a 34 cm row). A row: the points'
principal horizontal axis, its front toward the camera, length 1st-99th percentile, height 98th percentile above
the support; spine colours every 1.5 cm in the middle band, brightened out of the rack's shadow so the brightest
spines are paper white (`spine_albedo`). A round set: centre and radius.

**Looks**: `tint: "match"` scales the texture's mean to the photo's albedo of the furniture (`recon.materials`), read
from its best-seen face of group `ref`: a table's sides by default (its top faces the windows and carries glare and
the smear of what stood on it: matched to the top, the tint saturated at 2x and turned the wood orange), a counter's
top (`"ref": "top"`: its sides are cabinet doors).

**Glass**: the RTX renderer's OmniGlass MDL (`sim.usd.glass_material`); a UsdPreviewSurface with opacity renders as a
dark mirror there. Recon shells are thin-walled; kit glassware is solid, with faces pointing outward (`lathe` orients
them by the profile's turn: inward faces made every ray reflect totally, chrome) and 12 refraction bounces for a stack
of tumblers.

## Task objects against close views

Compare silhouettes, contact surfaces and texture from multiple scales. Exclude clipped channels from colour fitting. Reconstruct visible openings and retain functional clearances.

## Transparent bodies with opaque printing

Do not bake transmitted room/table RGB as the albedo of a transparent object.
Keep the source-authored transmission tint/roughness and derive opaque printing
separately. The agent can propose a print layer with:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src envs/recon/bin/python -m calibrated_scene_reconstruction.scene.objects.blender.optical_print \
  sessions/SESSION OBJECT REQUEST.json ATLAS.npz OUTPUT_DIRECTORY
```

The request names a registered `frame`, reviewed image `regions_xyxy`, and a
`basis`. Optional contrast, chroma, front-facing and depth tolerances describe
the source extraction. The atlas must preserve every physical triangle in
order. Front-surface ownership rejects mirrored rear-wall text; chroma and
reviewed regions can exclude transmitted objects. This experimental extractor
currently proposes dark printing. It does not recover arbitrary colour prints,
refraction, absolute reflectance or text hidden by an occluder.

The output is unapproved. Inspect its source regions, complementary opacity and
transmission maps, and native source-camera render before binding
`fix.look.optical_print`. That declaration names session-relative `opaque_mask`,
`transmission_mask`, `albedo` and `review`. The resolved review needs a basis,
`source_mesh_sha256`, `sha256` for all three maps and hashed `evidence_files`.
Changing the source, physical mesh, maps or review invalidates native task proof.
Opaque ink conservatively blocks source-observation transmission rays while
leaving the solid collision surface unchanged. Native MDL weight maps explicitly
read the red channel; default alpha on a grayscale PNG is not its weight.

`apps/scene/render_material_sweep.py` compares proposals against an unchanged
calibrated source crop. It writes candidates and provenance only; it never
approves appearance. Optional `--atlas` checks physical triangle correspondence.
Use actual Blender corner normals for final smooth shading. Preview-only
smoothing does not change exported geometry or establish a passing object loop.
Explicit `fit.pose_parameters` also controls initial yaw selection: excluding
`yaw_deg` retains the current-capture seed during an appearance-only round.

For an existing assembled USD, `apps/scene/review_usd_cameras.py` with
`--material-proposal REQUEST.json` compares explicit numeric inputs on existing
shaders in an anonymous session layer. The request needs a `basis` and `shaders`
mapping, each with `inputs`; connected inputs require a named `disconnect` list.
The source asset, geometry, physical parameters and light rig stay unchanged.
Compare more than one captured camera before adopting a material hypothesis.
In particular, a median source RGB is illuminated radiance, not identified
albedo; relighting it as albedo can double the captured shadow or colour cast.


### specified acceptance criteria

`task.json.acceptance` (version 1) allows for the declaration of required `criteria`, as well as `relaxations` listed by exact level names. Each relaxation must specify the range, reasons, and dependent input conditions. `scene.acceptance` retains `default_pass`, native measurement, and a separate `effective_pass`; the relaxation is only activated if all input conditions pass the declared checks and current evidence is preserved. Physical interaction conditions must come from Isaac 5.1 measurements. Unpecified default levels continue to execute. Physical invalidations, native errors, invalid evidence, and frozen assets are not within the scope of relaxations. It is allowed to run specified physical tests first, addressing preconditions where "default appearance failure blocks the test, while relaxations depend on that test"; this does not equal passing the level ahead of time.

When the user specifies that the Sharpa thumb and index finger should pick up soft materials by friction, it is necessary to test actual finger contact, approaching from above, target position, lifting, and holding. A zero-friction comparison for the same action is used to rule out false successes caused by hooking or intermingling. Node forces, welding, adhesion, or fabric position animation cannot replace this condition; therefore, no additional full robot autonomous planning is required. The dimensions of the complete material, rigidness, and deformation/collision effectiveness continue to be checked.

For observations with the `passive_shape` tag and an independent `part_prompt`, it is allowed to relax the capture/folding posture of the soft sheet after complete input conditions are met. Blender evidence still preserves the original whole object and component scores. Additionally, `source_pose_controls` is calculated in all original perspectives: only pixels from the independent source tags are excluded, and the geometry that is not relaxed is checked using the remaining real model surfaces. This additional control currently only supports native cloth links; it is not automatically extended to rigid, joint, or other soft materials. Exporting and OBJ levels re-verify the functional evidence bound to the current physical mesh. Relaxation is not enabled if the evidence is missing or expired.

The passing of input conditions only proves the condition, without directly approving the task or delivery. The original failure of the default source check is retained; any explicit condition that is successfully passed must be re-verified with current evidence during export.

The hand contact points should originate from points and normals on the actual collision surface. The CAD inverse kinematics of `pinch_plan` only generates candidate poses; freehand pre-checking and actual contact testing are still required. Before adapting the joint API, read the valid armature and speed limits from the source asset, and retain them explicitly after adaptation, then read back and verify them in the native articulation. Do not allow the default values of the new API to quietly replace the source parameters. Limited force drives, friction, and gains also need to be read back for verification. The target must be placed on the current actual material, and the clearance of the closing path should be screened by the actual hand geometry. Do not project material points onto fictional higher positions to create gripping areas. Candidates can be searched for limited wrist rotation angles around radial gripping axes at the same real material points, maintaining a towards-gripping direction, and the closing path is checked using the actual hand collision surface. This geometric screening does not prove reachability or successful gripping; wrist poses are executed through continuous rigid body rotation paths, and both positive and negative examples are recorded and compared with the actual rotation commands.

Material glide requires maintaining the internal and external proportions and clamping area during the analysis. This falls under task accessibility failures; even if sampling strain, penetration, and self-crossing checks are valid, the clamping conditions cannot be met. Geometric storage, natural morphology, and material assumptions need to be specified separately, and friction or stiffness cannot be determined directly from a single glide. Soft materials with creases also require distinguishing between captured postures and stress-free natural states. If rapid rebound occurs after release, which does not match the input, assumptions about storage, contact, and residual curvature must be considered, and stiffness cannot be increased alone. Residual curvature is a physical prior to be tested; it cannot be claimed as identified just because the task objective hopes for success. The current surface FEM approach only accepts equidistant `bend_reference` that has been independently compared with native materials; it must maintain the intrinsic metrics of the complete material, clarify sources and inference ranges, and pass new source responses, releases, and task checks. The entire captured shape cannot be directly locked as a natural state, nor can unverified independent bending angles be used as substitutes. If local self-crossing is detected during storage, record the failure and verify the actual triangles; numerical collision budgets can be compared using the same materials and paths. Isaac 107.3's `collisionPairUpdateFrequency` and `collisionIterationMultiplier` control contact pair updates and sub-collisions, as described in [NVIDIA Native Parameter Notes](https://docs.omniverse.nvidia.com/kit/docs/omni_physics/107.3/dev_guide/deformables_beta/physx_deformable_schema.html). Such comparisons do not identify real material parameters and cannot replace subsequent complete trajectory and task checks.

Geometric arrangements can utilize clearly defined numerical budgets for construction purposes, while maintaining accurate records of strain, self-intersections, solid penetration, and inherent errors. It only generates geometries that have not been approved, without qualifying the material responses. After reconstruction, it is necessary to re-evaluate the native materials, hand interactions, and mission-level challenges using formal configurations. Construction trajectories cannot be reused across these levels. `preparation_geometry.downward_hits` uses actual static triangular queries to determine the placement of surfaces, preserving holes and slopes. When there are no intersections, missing parts are clearly indicated, and the bottom surface of the bounding box is not used as a substitute. For materials with limited area, multiple queries are required, and these geometric calculations must never replace actual collisions and mission checks. If multiple material regions do not overlap, it does not mean that their control targets are not conflicting with each other. Folding materials should be handled in groups, with 3D layer offsets preserved. `transport_centres` uses non-scaled orthogonal transformations, ensuring that different folding layers are not compressed onto a single central line. Before construction, the spacing between target groups must be checked. After construction, the strain, interlayer contacts, and self-intersections of the actual materials must still be monitored. When folding is necessary, the curve length must be constrained along with the length of the entire material and the height of the cavity. It is important to check whether the material retains its gripping position after release in all required states. Curved folding layers correspond to different arc lengths, so it is not possible to force all layers to use the same material for different radii. `sliding_stations` provides proposals for material positions with locally parallel curves. The shear, deformation, and contact of the material must still be checked independently. `release_ramp_s` allows for gradual removal of temporary constraints, with release occurring in stages across different areas. Reports should include both the internal forces within the controller and the actual forces applied. This method does not alter the parameters of the actual materials, nor can unleased states be used to replace the initial state that will be delivered.

The `construction_tracking` function in the report records the nominal path, the controlled target after speed limit, and the positions of the actual material areas, distinguishing between path lag and actual tracking errors. The recorded path does not indicate that the material has been placed; first, the tracking is checked, and then the behavior after unloading is explained. `grip_budget` proposes a limited tool force and gain based on the bending load of gravity and `D*w/r²`, and reports whether the tool's upper limit or the stretching magnitude is insufficient. This is only an initial estimate of the controller, not a readback of contact forces or identification of material parameters. If the initial state depends on clamping, the required contact must be established first, followed by gradual unloading. The maintenance of subsequent states and the actual task are still independently tested. The stiffness, mass, or friction of the object cannot be modified due to insufficient tool force estimates.

If the material loses its required properties after reasonable storage and maintains a straight natural form, it is possible to compare the residual curvature assumptions of different material axes and retain the comparison between straight and reverse configurations. The static source diagram can only constrain the combination of material history, contact, and elasticity; it cannot directly assume the visible suspension posture as the recognized natural form. Each natural curvature proposal must maintain a triangular internal metric, re-examine release, support changes, complete tasks, and physical prior sensitivity; such comparisons do not prove the true material history, nor do they relax any acceptance criteria in advance. The organizer can also only drive joints while retaining empty `grips` to isolate the material response after mechanism movement. This comparison does not apply fabric organizing forces; it is still necessary to record the state of the hinge drive without external forces after release. It cannot replace actual finger gripping or directly approve tasks.

The complete soft sheet is not a default uniform strip. `material_chart_rotation_rad` only rotates the two-dimensional coordinates of the editing area, leaving the original intrinsic metrics, area, and node states unchanged. It can represent folding or diagonal nesting proposals in different directions. `nearby_patch` searches for existing, non-overlapping material regions within a limited distance, unable to fill in cut edges or holes. When calculating exposed volume, the difference between the source contour, material area, and visible length is preserved, rather than directly inferring the proportion of the entire material from an outer length. In case of construction anomalies, save `partial_result.json` and `partial_states.npz`; they are clearly incomplete and inoperable. Retain the actual tracking data before failure, without replacing the complete release trajectory.

The time step for real-hand pre-checking and the PGS solver must be consistent with the object experiment. Empty hand movements with different numerical configurations cannot be used to approve actual contact. The independently controlled finite-force fixture also needs to be checked for stability; `force_fixture.implicit_pd` provides backward Euler spring/damping forces, and the caller still needs to limit the total force and explicitly compensate for gravity. It does not modify the object material or directly write positions. Fixture stability does not mean contact coupling stability. Actual strain, collision, and zero-friction responses still need to be checked. The official frequency cannot be reduced solely based on contactless material tests.

For internal elastic diagnostic, the option `--linear-solver sparse_direct` is available. It solves the same positive definite linear equation as the convergent CG solver, with residuals and momentum checks remaining unchanged. This is a numerical solution option, not an increase in material stiffness. After reducing the time step or changing the linear solver, the results need to be regenerated. The pass marked results cannot be reused.

The in-mesh stiffness, bending, and contact friction of the cloth must be checked separately. The written friction coefficient cannot prove effective interlayer friction. The cloth link in `mechanisms.json` can declare `required_contact_effects`, with values being `rigid_friction`, `self_friction`, or `surface_pair_friction`. After declaration, `material_response.run` will read `agent/contact_response.json` to find the current session QA paths and SHA256 for `zero` and `nominal` based on the link, prior case, and effect. Tasks with missing, expired, inconsistent configurations, or those that do not exhibit the desired effects will not pass. Existing tasks without this declaration will remain subject to the original rules.

`contact_response.compare` checks the executed code, native friction readings, actual initial conditions, materials, loads, and complete valid trajectories. It compares the responses of zero-friction free sliding versus normal friction. The results are categorized as `supported`, `unsupported_at_tested_profile`, or `inconclusive`. Invalid contacts or expired code indicate insufficient evidence, and thus cannot be used to claim that a simulator does not support a particular effect. The determination applies only to the recorded backend version, representation, mesh, and solution configuration. It does not equate to true material identification, source appearance, or task completion. Independent segments within the same fabric are used only for isolating self-contact comparisons and cannot be delivered as a complete wet wipe model.

The native contact comparison inputs are `apps/scene/check_surface_pair_friction.py` and `apps/scene/check_particle_contact_friction.py`. The two runs use a separate `--output`; the `--support` and other inputs remain unchanged, with only `--friction 0` being altered to a normal coefficient. Then, `contact_response.compare(zero_result, nominal_result)` is invoked. The task level also checks the materials in use, time steps, collision radii, mesh, and solution budget. Particle run configurations that are not yet qualified will fail explicitly, and no FEM evidence will be applied.

For particle materials, it’s also necessary to check for issues related to contact sampling. The fact that vertices don’t penetrate each other doesn’t mean that staggering or rotating layers is effective. The `--grid-offset-fraction .5` parameter can be used to adjust the offset between particles and triangles. It’s also important to check for self-intersection, strain, and finite thickness intervals in real-world triangles. If a particle still gets stuck due to grid irregularities after friction is set to zero, it won’t pass the test for friction-based interactions. The native particle material doesn’t exhibit the expected behavior when friction is set to zero; therefore, we can’t directly use the FEM method for clearing friction. Reducing the base friction of particles and compensating for particle-particle friction scales requires separate tests. Additionally, this method can only be used to isolate finger friction effects when other rigid body contacts have been resolved. Global material changes cannot replace local interventions that involve fingers still touching the bottle.

`particle_lattice.chart` generates balanced diagonal springs for existing regular material meshes, without filling in voids. The explicit `spring_model: balanced_square_lattice` must be validated using per-cell topology. Its small strain Poisson's ratio is 1/3, and distance bending is not equivalent to the bending stiffness of a specified continuum. Spring constants cannot be approved simply by formula conversion; it is necessary to run the `check_particle_membrane.py` for native tensile comparison. The `--constitutive implicit_shell` and `check_particle_flexure.py` for this entry are diagnostic checks for internal elastic forces and Isaac particle integration/contact coupling: disable and read back the native springs, apply only physical internal impulses, and do not write positions. It has not yet been registered as a delivery backend; membrane internal bending, conservation, contact, prior sensitivity, and actual tasks must all be processed separately and cannot be replaced by a single tensile test.

In Isaac 107.3 FEM, the units of `elasticityDamping` and `bendDamping` are `1/s`. For details, see the [parameter documentation of the corresponding version](https://docs.omniverse.nvidia.com/kit/docs/omni_physics/107.3/dev_guide/deformables_beta/physx_deformable_schema.html). We will maintain compatibility with existing unit labels and report any corrections. Do not alter the values or freeze the scene based on these labels.

The initial placement relationships confirmed by the user must also be checked. For example, the `initial_support` of a cloth link can be declared with `link`, `relation: above_unthreaded`, `surface_region`, the unit `up` in model coordinates, and evidence `source`. `surface_region` can optionally specify all upward surfaces, the lowest or highest horizontal support areas; pallets with protruding edges should explicitly select the actual bottom support area, and the edge plane should not be extended into the interior cavity to avoid false reports. `support_route.above_unthreaded` checks whether the material within the support range falls below the actual surface; outer overhang materials are not subject to this constraint. Holes are retained in the collision mesh without adding new entities, tightening of perforations, or any fixing forces. This initial geometric check is incorporated into Blender's candidate admission, actual hand testing, and task start state; it does not equal proven support forces, subsequent maintenance, or extraction.

The contact mesh encryption can be performed using `triangle_refine.refine`. The shared edges are subdivided according to the original topology, while maintaining the original surfaces, holes, material coordinates, and UV seams. Quality cannot be interpolated based on vertex attributes. After encryption, it is necessary to recheck the finite thickness intervals: materials excluded from the coarse mesh may cover up actual interlayer contacts. The nearest point query for tiny triangles is first normalized by local edge lengths, and then converted back to meters to avoid missing geometric tolerances in the library. Geometric repairs use all candidate pairs that violate the interval, and truncated few examples from the report cannot be used as optimization input. Repair intervals may increase local material stretching; therefore, it is necessary to recheck complete materials, thickness, self-intersections, solid penetration, and initial support relationships. If necessary, alternate between material and contact projections. All projections only generate candidate initial states; they do not fix the fabric at runtime and cannot replace the original physical or real-finger task verification.

The experimental particle internal elastic adapter can temporarily isolate effects related to gravity, self-contact, and collisions between particles and rigid objects during the native reset process. After the process ends, the status should be checked to ensure that no changes have occurred. The collision shapes of dynamic SDF rigid bodies remain intact; it’s not possible to restore contact state by simply changing the simulation shape flag. Records of previous interactions, native error detection, and actual contact verification must all be preserved. The material dissipation and reloading processes associated with this adapter are still under evaluation and have not yet been approved for use as fully functional assets.

The large folding candidates also need to distinguish between internal elastic predictions and the state after native contact. Only a decrease in total energy is checked, which cannot prove that local materials are not overstretched. Experimental internal elastic updates can iteratively minimize the nonlinear energy objective of the backward Euler method; each tangent solution retains six rigid body motion constraints, uses energy line search, and reports both linear residual and nonlinear gradient residuals. If there is no convergence, the advancement of native time steps is rejected, and failures cannot be hidden by scaling stiffness, rewriting positions, or relaxing material thresholds. Even if the solution converges, actual materials, self-contact, solid contact, and physical checks for the task must still be passed.

In nearly static conditions, the reference force of the relative gradient may be close to zero. At this time, it is also necessary to check the Newton position correction amount that has not been shortened through line search, divide it by the edge length scale of the inherent material mesh, and use the numerical tolerance of the solver; record which convergence criterion was used and its actual value. It is not possible to pretend convergence with very short line search steps, nor can the strain permissible values for the task be used to replace the numerical convergence requirements. The non-linear initial guess retains the current rigid body velocity, starting from the current material shape; the external boundary impulse remains within the same moment of inertia target, avoiding predicting free motion as a long strip before converging to another local minimum.

The `--linear-solver cuda_direct` option can be used to utilize cuDSS0.5 to solve the same positive definite equations, with the same six rigid body motion constraints. If this option is explicitly selected, failures due to missing libraries, incompatible ABI, numerical decomposition errors, or excessive residuals will not occur. The solver will not be automatically changed during execution. You can install the library using the following command in your workspace: `uv pip install --python /path/to/isaac/python --target envs/numerics --no-deps nvidia-cudss-cu12==0.5.0.16`. Alternatively, you can use `SCENE_RECONSTRUCTION_CUDSS_LIBRARY` to point to a compatible library. When installing the library, keep its own license file. For more information, refer to [NVIDIA installation instructions](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cudss/getting_started.html). The SHA256 hash of the adapter source code and actual library files should be verified. Only numerical interfaces are checked for consistency between GPUs and CPUs. Other tests, such as material, contact, and task-related tests, still need to be run again.

The condition that a thin layer with declared thickness can only be admitted as an entry condition when the surface is not penetrated by the medium. The initial state also checks whether the distance from the medium to the actual solid surface allows for a half-thickness, and records the insufficient amount and sample count. This condition is verified together with the inter-layer thickness, self-crossing, and tray placement relationship; the cache is checked while binding the mesh content and the current condition, and the condition will be rechecked if modified. The particle model can also declare geometric thickness, but it cannot use this to treat the continuum modulus as a spring constant.

A sufficient particle coverage does not necessarily indicate initial compatibility. `cloth_sampling.initial_overlaps` additionally checks for numerical envelope overlaps at non-local material points and limits the query scale; it records an explicit reference neighborhood exclusion range. This is a conservative geometric screening method and cannot replace the native pair filter with actual contact verification. Material thickness, particle numerical radius, and rigid body contact intervals are recorded separately, and insufficient sampling is not compensated by thickening materials. Pose proposals can use both `MaterialBounds` and `ParticleEnvelopes`; they do not change the inherent material coordinates or apply runtime forces. Complete strain, thickness, self-intersection, solid gap, and native verification still need to be verified. When changing the rigid body closed reference pose to accommodate materials trapped in gaps, it is necessary to maintain the shape and connections of the rigid body, clearly document the assumptions, and re-perform the source graph appearance check. A pose cannot be approved solely based on contact compatibility.

The native initialization also requires separate verification of material neighborhood filtering. The installed version of the particle comparison shows that when cooking directly in the folded posture, simply changing `restPoints` does not result in the expected material neighborhood exclusion relationship. `apps/scene/check_particle_rest_filter.py` changes the cooking spacing, declared material spacing, and the method for setting the initial state before timing, to record the native contact response. This is an interface diagnostic, not related to fabric materials or task qualification. The numerical thresholds apply only to the recorded radii and versions of this comparison.

The experimental `--cook-material-rest` option first applies the “cooking” process based on the inherent material coordinates, maintaining any contact with the object, isolating gravity, and preserving the properties of rigid bodies. Subsequently, the `InitialMaterialPose` function is used to set the initial model state at physical time t0. The native `reset` mechanism has its own warming-up period; it’s essential to record this time as the starting point for physical time t0, as further resets after that point would be invalid. Before initialization, the position must be consistent with the cooking geometry, and the velocity must be zero. Hash values of both sets of coordinates must be recorded, and any errors in the read-back data cannot be retried. All contacts and gravitational forces must be restored before formal recording. During operation, tracking of position targets is prohibited. This method only corrects issues related to initialization semantics; it does not address problems such as overlapping objects, penetration, material distortion, or incorrect folding structures. These checks must still be performed independently before formal delivery to the backend.

Method review cannot simply increase the number of levels. The `deformable_method_plan` input in modeling specifies grid requirements based on the actual backend: for surface FEM, it checks the convergence of triangular face contact and material response, without inheriting the refinement caused by particle ball coverage; for particle method, it requires compatibility between particle coverage and initial numerical envelope. Both methods retain true finite thickness, complete material dimensions, and independent physical checks. The experimental external force elastic adapter does not replace the formal route during material dissipation, contact, task execution, and export reloading verification; experimental candidates cannot quietly switch to another backend to pass the task.

When local repairs fail, first review the hidden folding structure, support relationships, initial resilience, and the constraint expressions of the repairer. Then decide whether to change material assumptions. The geometric proposed rigid body clearance constraints use vertices, midpoints of edges, and centers of faces, consistent with independent checks; constraining only vertices will miss contact between small bodies and interior triangles. Temporary contact checks do not prove the stability of complete folded assets. First verify the initial state and release without external manipulation, then perform actual gripping and match friction counterexamples, and finally verify the derived overload.

Automatic finger pre-checking must read the `simulation.json` of the task and the torque limits in the hand plan. It cannot use another set of default frequencies to generate evidence that cannot be used for task acceptance later. Explicitly inputting allowed level relaxations still retains the original failure; geometric or original physical anomalies cannot be changed to appearance issues that can be relaxed.

For folds that are already within the legal strain range, it is not necessary to minimize the clearance to zero strain. `cloth_pose_projection.repair_clearances` provides offline contact inequality repairs with displacement budgets, where each step is still constrained by independent material validity and non-intersecting conditions. It does not change material coordinates or impose runtime position targets; achieving local linear convergence cannot replace complete triangular face clearances, supports, source graphs, and native inspections.

The material assumptions in the native setting are not identified results. After geometric and numerical states are validated, the unmanipulated forms and motion responses from the original video can be used to compare material assumptions within the original range. Unidentified parameters and uncertainties must be retained, and true stiffness or friction cannot be inferred solely from task success. The explicit material property scheme for native experiments and `--rigid-collision-plan` accept the isolated assumptions under the current session's QA, preserving their content and hash values. Such diagnostic results and those from non-current formal checkers cannot be registered as input acceptance evidence. After adopting parameters or collision representations, it is necessary to re-run matching materials, contacts, tasks, and export reloaded checks. For rigid bodies, `link_physics.<link>.collision_approximation` can be explicitly set to `sdf` or `convexDecomposition`, with the default being SDF. Convex decomposition cannot be automatically approved: it is still necessary to check the geometric errors, cavities, and complete interactions of the actual collision approximation. Overfilling collision bodies cannot be relied upon to maintain fabric integrity.

After the shape of small objects is matched, it is still necessary to check whether UV unwrapping and native collisions lose small features. Texture unwrapping calculates UVs in normalized coordinates, and the returned geometry retains the original metric coordinates; `atlas_geometry.json` records the surface area lost due to zero-area UVs. Appearance comparison uses depth testing and per-pixel perspective UV sampling. It is not allowed for backfaces to cover front faces, nor can this type of unlit reprojection be treated as actual scene rendering.

The thickness structure of the same rigid body can be explicitly grouped using `link_physics.<link>.collision_groups`: each group specifies a `name`, source `materials`, and `approximation`. All original triangular faces must be assigned exactly once. `convexHull` or `convexDecomposition` with the full `cooking` parameter only generates collisions corresponding to the source faces in that group; it does not change the rigid body's ownership, mass, appearance, or add any connections. The `normalize_coordinates: true` option for a single convex hull can normalize local coordinates before baking, and then use USD transformations to restore the original size. It is necessary to measure the actually baked shape and verify the dimensions after transformation; you cannot rely solely on the USD request values. Grouping does not guarantee that cavities still exist, nor can it replace checks for insertion, release, initial stability, and export overloads. `apps/scene/inspect_convex_collision.py` saves the transformations from native convex hulls to the world. The engine rejects convex decomposition parameters; even a warning will cause the collision accuracy check to fail.

The initial state check for a rigid body must cover all pairs of parts that may collide, not just the parts with the container. `check_rigid_assembly.py` uses real closed triangular faces to check for bidirectional penetration before starting dynamics, and saves the `initial_rigid_state.json` file. If it fails, the "first settle then check" alternative acceptance criterion is not executed. This is a limited sampling inspection; it cannot prove the absence of in-plane intersections, nor can it replace native collision and task checks. Assemblies with joints or pairs prohibited from colliding require a checker that can explicitly handle their collision relationships.

The internal pixels of transparent objects cannot be directly treated as ordinary camera rays. `refracted_rays` provides geometric diagnostics for rays passing through two interfaces in opaque glass; the thickness, refractive index, reflection, and source pixel identity remain uncertain. First, verify the edges of labels, reflections, and actual internal objects, then interpret differences in internal and external contours. Do not add bends, clamps, or fixed connections to straight parts just to pass a physical inspection.

`scene.objects.blender.optical_print`'s `mode: opaque_patch` accepts `polygons_xy` from the source image, retaining the entire opaque label and its original image color. By default, `ink` mode only processes ink. Labels are applied only to visible translucent material surfaces; the background table and sticks cannot become part of the glass surface's texture. Both proposals require review by the source image and native rendering. `render_material_sweep.py --object-only` can inspect materials before assembling the complete scene, using neutral lighting and disabling dynamics. However, this cannot be used to approve the overall appearance or task feasibility of the complete scene. The inspection results are documented, and no process exit code is used to replace the quality level.

The correspondence between the tips of repeated thin rods and the entire axis must be verified separately. `landmark_sets.triangulate_lines` directly minimizes the pixel distance from each source line segment to the projection axis; the endpoints of the lines do not necessarily correspond to the same physical endpoint. `triangulate_line_sets` can jointly match independent lines that vary in order per frame and are partially obscured, avoiding forced connections using incorrect rod tip identities. The input lines must first undergo verification for source image identity and repeated segmentation; reports should retain the error and matching relationships for each input perspective. They only propose infinite axes, without identifying rod lengths, radii, hidden parts, or supports. Line segment proposals cannot replace the entire contour cache, nor can they approve of the physical initial state.

The point connections, boundary connections, and finite-area bonds of flexible parts need to be distinguished. When a surface is declared as a clamping or bonding surface, `surface_bindings[].clamp` should clearly specify `minimum_width_m` and `minimum_area_m2`. The checker measures the triangular surfaces of materials that are truly completely within the connection; individual points or a row of collinear nodes cannot replace finite bonding surfaces. It only checks the geometry of the connection area; actual support, contact, and bidirectional forces still need to be verified.

When complete flexible assembly anomalies occur, first check gravity-free rest, free fall, support contact, and independent force application in sequence. Maintain shape in gravity-free conditions; incorrect free fall or table contact does not prove correctness. Material comparison requires distinguishing between external force interfaces, connections, solvers, and discrete errors; achieving compliance cannot be achieved by simply increasing stiffness or reducing actual mass. `check_surface_extension.py` It uses initial small strains to induce free stretching, without writing node positions or velocities after reset. This can complement the stretching comparison affected by external forces. It still involves numerical material inspection; it cannot identify the actual material of objects in the video, nor can it replace full scene and export reloading verification.

### Semantic and soft body accuracy

Semantic inspection covers all reconstructed objects, including furniture such as tables. In addition to categories and the number of components, the inspection also includes layout, repetitive structures, symmetry, connections, and support relationships. Symmetry is a structural prerequisite that requires review by the source image; it is not required for all objects to be symmetrical. Unjustified asymmetrical structures cannot be approved based solely on contour scores. The SUP level for tables now includes reading the current semantic information, reviewing textureless structures, and measuring the declared geometric layout.

Flexible bodies are accepted by default based on `task_and_semantics`: the input should be aligned as much as possible, with correct semantics and structure, reasonable material behavior, and compliance with contact, stability, and actual tasks. Precise local deformation, strain, and material response errors are retained for diagnostic purposes; it is not required that every non-critical value be perfectly aligned. `task.json.flexible_accuracy: quantitative` can require strict runtime response checks; the component's `response_accuracy: quantitative` retains independent quantitative physical testing. The shape, scale accuracy, and task-related contact requirements of rigid bodies should not be compromised, and flexible components cannot be replaced by rigid shells. Task success does not equal the identification of true material parameters.

In qualitative mode, flexible details that are explicitly marked as non-task-critical should be clearly identified. During runtime, review prompts for connection and contact errors should be set at 10% of the rotational invariance span of these details. The combined numerical error should not exceed 2mm. If the error exceeds the prompt line, the actual state must be reviewed, but attempts to perform tasks should not be prevented solely because of this. Initial modeling interlacing, non-finite/collapsing materials, and topological errors are still not allowed. Local self-contact and precise responses should retain their original diagnostics, and semantic and physical rationality must be reviewed in conjunction with the actual simulation state. Secondary qualitative flexible bodies require `self_contact_recorded`; original results with intersections should not be falsely labeled as `no_self_intersection`. Task-critical flexible body contacts and all rigid body contacts remain subject to the same requirements. Small structures with dense bonding can propose approximate composite structures, but visible geometry must be retained, and the approximation must be justified by independent material/load error tests. Structures cannot be changed to rigid bodies merely due to solver failures. Semantic review should take into account original simulation evidence, separately bind candidate solutions and evidence hashes, and prevent review conclusions from invalidating the simulation records being reviewed.

The native motion fails when non-finite poses occur. `native_assay` retains the rigid body pose before failure, actual soft body nodes, external forces, contacts, and readings of independent rigid bodies. The trajectory cannot be completed with the last normal pose. First, reproduce the main task, then arrange a complete comparison of secondary objects to avoid repeating long auxiliary tests each time. `apps/scene/check_assembly_motion.py` can perform short-term numerical diagnostics using the current candidate setup/probe with hashing. It does not provide source diagrams or complete task acceptance results; the output is always `diagnostic_only`, and it cannot serve as evidence of passing S5/S6. Priority should be given to checking the numerical stability of time steps and connections under conditions where geometry, mass, and materials remain unchanged. After selecting a configuration, the entire process, physical prior checks, and export reloading must still be run again.

When merging soft bodies with the same material, it is also necessary to check whether the native mass distribution is compatible. Different ratios of the area of independent surfaces to the number of nodes can alter the true mass of each surface in a uniform node mass model; this cannot be circumvented by reducing the number of solver objects without checking `surface_mass.distribution`. For small load material screening under rigid body approximation, actual beam responses of the cross-section can be used as references, maintaining cavity assumptions, material ranges, and cross-sectional refinement results. This reference is a conditional estimate, and engineering priors should not be treated as measured true materials.

The candidate adhesion experiment uses `tasks.rigid.native_nesting.run`: the receiving container is fixed, and the moving container is placed only once before timing begins. After that, limited lateral/angular force tools, gravity, and axial damping are applied. All tool forces are turned off during the resting phase, and a bounded tension is used during the separation phase, with a limited rotational moment also declared separately. Normal insertion and free-falling impacts are different experiments; the final velocity of free fall under pure damping can be declared using `insertion_terminal_speed_m_s`, and the insertion duration using `insertion_duration_s`. It does not set the running speed or position, and cannot circumvent fixture issues by modifying object friction or mass. Reports should retain actual damping, external forces, contact forces, penetration, and trajectory details for each phase. Material prior boundaries, source graphs, complete scenarios, and exported loadings still need to be individually verified; a candidate pass will not be recorded in `task_check.json`.

`check_candidate_mechanism.py` binds runtime inputs with the hash of loaded modules. Do not modify these files during execution; any changes detected will cause `changed_inputs.json` to be written and the round to fail. Errors are recorded in `failure.json`, and no process exit code or rendered image can replace the acceptance result.

The independent soft body probe uses the same secondary soft body sampling policy as the scenario. The allowable sampling intervals for each triangular face are calculated based on the understood bound object and task configuration. The original restrictions still apply to the main task contact and quantitative components. When sampling approximation is performed, `physical_coverage_pass: false` is retained, indicating that continuous contact has not been verified. However, actual self-contact, entity contact, static states, source graphs, and task checks must still be executed.

Translucent materials require a distinction between clear refraction and internal scattering, rather than simply reducing opacity. Blender Principled's non-zero `Subsurface Weight`, `Subsurface Radius × Scale`, and anisotropy are passed to Isaac OmniSurface through `assets.subsurface_optics`. Scattering lengths are stored in meters and then converted to the USD length unit. Materials with zero weight retain their original export behavior. Unsupported texture-driven scattering parameters will result in a clear error, and default constants cannot be silently taken. The correspondence between the two rendering models is a candidate for appearance, not an identified true optical parameter. Multiple source images must still be compared under the same geometry, camera, and lighting conditions. Density, friction, or other mechanical parameters will not be modified. The parameter units are based on the [NVIDIA OmniSurface documentation](https://docs.omniverse.nvidia.com/materials-and-rendering/latest/templates/parameters/OmniSurface_Subsurface.html).

When estimating circular edges from segmented contours, the cut-off edges of the image and the boundaries of known occluded areas are not considered as object edges. `scene.objects.source_circle.visible_boundary` is only used to initialize the extraction of visible arcs and record manually removed pixels; it cannot be used to delete the original image, observe the entire object, or result in failed acceptance. The frame-by-frame angle fitting of movable parts is also only for motion diagnosis, and geometric or segmentation errors cannot be automatically interpreted as real motion. Independent observation constraints are established for different states only when there is evidence from the source image supporting state changes.

The cloth intermediate file must store both vertices, actual triangular faces, and original material coordinates. When reloading the mesh, use `cloth_rest.triangulate_grid(..., faces=saved_faces)` to maintain the checked surfaces and spring connections; changing the quadrilateral diagonals will alter the surfaces, so old thickness or contact results cannot be reused. If the old file contains only vertices, the original topology must be restored and rechecked, or the affected checks must be performed again.

For repeated materials on the desktop, you cannot simply select the source image based on the projected area: larger unobstructed areas may still suffer from out-of-focus issues or motion blur. It is recommended to compare texture candidates with the same physical scale of the original image, while maintaining occlusion checks, unique spot checks, and original cropping. The `--source-frame` parameter in `scene.support.texture_patch`, combined with `--selection-basis`, can specify the current input frame that the agent has already reviewed. This parameter only selects material sampling images and does not delete the reconstructed acceptance frames. When reducing the texture block size, the actual repetition cycle should be updated accordingly; wood grain patterns should not be enlarged. The results must still be compared using the same geometry, camera, and lighting in the native renderer.
