# Reconstruct an environment

Choose the extent from the task. A workspace-only twin needs supports, object placements and their robot-relative scale. A room also needs source-view coverage, depth ordering and support for nearby furniture. Use the registered scene frame throughout. Reuse the reviewed task support and protect the frozen task zone.

Keep one room target and one [revision](../../../end_to_end/docs/reconstruction.md) for its authored geometry, visual layers and composition. Choose metric capture and room tools for measured placement. Use Blender for editable geometry, recovered mesh for observed structure, and a captured Gaussian field where image coverage supports novel views. In hybrid scenes check the coverage and visibility of each actual layer. Mark inferred completion separately and check overlap, holes, floaters, contact, material and source-view appearance in the composed scene.

When reviewing a spatial cousin or task-area clutter, check [room parallax, furniture access and obstacle clearance](references/experience/spatial-cousin-review.md).

Use [interactive composition](../compose-interactive-scene/SKILL.md) for runtime packages and [Gaussian preparation](../prepare-gaussian-background/SKILL.md) for field inputs. Verify the requested simulator can load every representation. Preserve independent interactive participants, colliders and their contact tests. Record the view and motion evidence used to accept the room and retain a failed comparison when revising it.
