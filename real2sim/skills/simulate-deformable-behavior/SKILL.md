# Deformable behavior

## Backend selection

Follow the user's requested backend. When none is specified, default to **PhysX for cloth, garments and volumetric soft bodies**. If the user explicitly requests Newton, use Newton and its appropriate solver. Existing tool availability is not a reason to override this choice. Identify a missing adapter or unsupported feature explicitly; do not silently change backend. For PhysX garments, read [the garment guidance](physx-garments.md).

The native VBD tools described below are the Newton-specific route, not the default backend policy for all deformables.

Choose the physical representation before the material: a rope needs a continuous centerline and radius; tissue, cloth and bags need a manifold midsurface and thickness; a cushion or rubber object needs a closed solid and tetrahedra. A filled cushion is not automatically a homogeneous solid: distinguish the cover/filling approximation from measured material behavior. Use `simulate-slender-bodies` for the existing source-bound rope route. Existing cloth generation and final acceptance remain available through `review-deformation` and the normal simulator route.

`tetrahedralize_control_mesh` and inline `run_deformable_behavior` requests are optional diagnostics. Supply a workspace-relative request JSON and an `assumptions` string. For behavior testing also choose `render_video`: false returns seven sampled renders; true renders every retained frame and registers a movie directly for `inspect_video`. Diagnostics retain their inputs and results under the run's `behavior_tools/` directory and leave existing asset acceptance unchanged. A source-bound surface request can explicitly select final review with `record_asset_evidence=true`, as described below. Neither branch certifies a robot task. Detailed request fields and CLI equivalents are in [the behavior interface](../../docs/DEFORMABLE_BEHAVIOR.md).

- For tetrahedralization, author a closed, consistently wound, nonintersecting triangle control boundary. The conversion returns `geometry.json` with explicit vertices, triangles and tetrahedra. Preserve the detailed visual surface separately; the existing surface binding can drive it from the simulated boundary. Tetrahedralization is not visual reconstruction and does not repair arbitrary overlapping solids.
- For shell/solid behavior, author explicit geometry, material assumptions and a test. Native VBD advances unconstrained degrees of freedom; sphere/box probes impose physical contacts, while selected particle grasps impose explicit boundary conditions and can release. Grasp motion alone cannot prove contact, and dragging every vertex cannot prove deformation.
- Let the object settle before a deliberate interaction. Choose regional measurement windows for a baseline, response and optional recovery. Select informative ungrasped vertices when judging deformation propagation. The tool reports signed motion along the supplied axis, element validity and stretch. A passing finite-state check does not establish the intended behavior.
- When contact is important, a collision-disabled probe control can distinguish contact-induced deformation from gravity settling. For grasp-controlled tests, also inspect ungrasped regions and post-release behavior. Test fixtures and grasp trajectories remain distinct from the delivered object's passive degrees of freedom.
- Review returned images. For fast motion or complex contact, consider `render_video=true` and inspect the registered video. The standalone replay CLI can also render the retained full cache. The diagnostic renderer shows the actual control boundary, not a fabricated appearance or replacement simulation. Failure reports and finite caches are useful feedback; repair the original geometry/control/material and rerun.

Repair in an evidence-driven order: feasible motion and contact setup; geometry and boundary conditions; time step and solver budget; then the constitutive model when the observed behavior requires it. Do not raise validity tolerances to conceal penetrations. World-space normal reversal is legitimate folding, whereas intrinsic area collapse or tetrahedral inversion is a defect.

For lifted thin sheets, inspect temporal behavior separately from stretch and clearance. Watch the initial settling, departure from a support or opening, and a sufficiently long stationary-grasp hold. Persistent large flutter in still air can invalidate an otherwise intact, collision-free result. Compare free-region speed, lateral excursion and decay against the actual controller and force history; do not infer a numerical instability from a single moving fold or assume that a light sheet must flutter strongly. Verify the runtime interpolation before blaming abrupt keyframes. Check effective membrane/bending damping, initial equilibrium, contact/self-contact and solver convergence as competing explanations. Record assumptions when tuning dissipation; use actual resimulation, without filtering cached motion, hiding intervals or pinning the whole surface. No universal quiet-motion threshold applies to wind-driven or deliberately shaken tasks.

A shared-scene adapter must preserve or explicitly report solver/contact assumptions from standalone testing: nominally identical geometry and material are insufficient if the contact distances or solver formulation changed.

Before borrowing a stable example's coefficients, check its units and installed solver version. Newton VBD changed damping from stiffness-relative multipliers to absolute coefficients; centimeter-scale examples also need dimensional conversion. Read the matching implementation before interpreting a `kd` value. When a fresh run disagrees with a retained success, establish a current matching baseline before attributing the regression to scene composition. Appropriate internal membrane/bending dissipation is a material assumption to validate, not automatically a defect; it does not justify filtering motion or disabling required contacts.

When a smooth lift or hold still produces unexplained shaking, read the `review-dynamic-contact` skill and its `lift-motion.md` resource. Task-scoped native code can inspect the retained controller and trajectories directly; do not wait for a new object-specific check tool.

On curved supports, particle clearance alone can leave a coarse triangle cutting into the collider between its vertices. Refine the contact surface and choose numerical contact radius consistently with that mesh and physical thickness. The sphere audit subtracts half shell thickness, and the diagnostic renderer centers Solidify thickness on the physical midsurface. Small colored support flecks through a sheet are visual contact failures even when a loose numerical tolerance passes; inspect the actual video/crops and repair geometry/contact setup. Do not hide a collider or repaint the exposed region.

The current shell uses elastic stretch/bending; the solid uses Newton's native stable Neo-Hookean FEM. These are effective parameter assumptions, not real-world system identification. Tissue drape does not imply hard-paper crease retention: permanent folds need plastic bending, which this adapter does not currently implement. Winding diagnostics do not prove knot manipulation or continuous-time collision freedom.

Research inspiration: [CoDimRecon](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.36024v3), especially dimensionality-specific geometry and behavioral revision. Its IPC-family backend is separate from our Newton adapter. Native implementation references: [Newton source and examples](https://github.com/newton-physics/newton), [MeshPy](https://github.com/inducer/meshpy).

For thin sheets passing narrow openings, the optional solver.full_surface_contact=true uses Newton’s native edge/face contact path with supported analytic rigid probes. This is distinct from particle-only contact and is useful when walls can be crossed between vertices. Retained triangle/box SAT checks and final-region projection bounds provide additional evidence; they do not replace video review or certify continuous-time contact. Preserve adequate physical substeps when changing output FPS.

For a saved Blender surface and original-material replay, read [the source-surface branch](source-surface.md). source_attempt_id plus control/visual/fixed-fixture ownership allows optional native surface delivery through the same tool; record_asset_evidence explicitly selects it. Inline diagnostic requests keep their existing role.
