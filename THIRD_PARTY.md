# Third-party components

The project [MIT license](LICENSE) covers the authors' code and documentation. Source checkouts also include materials with their own terms.

- The modified Blender Cell Fracture add-on includes its GPL and source. The Cosmos Predict2 compatibility patch includes the upstream Apache license. The [reconstruction notices](real2sim/THIRD_PARTY.md) describe the files, versions and terms, including those for a separately fetched SAM 3D Objects installation.
- The [robot inventory](real2sim/assets/robots/manifest.json) records model provenance. Use the notices supplied with each distributed model.
- Optional simulators, submodules, grasp solvers and model weights are supplied by their respective providers. Follow the terms for the specific revision and assets you obtain. See the [grasp integration instructions](real2sim/docs/robots/grasping.md).
- The team created the [reconstruction examples](examples/README.md#asset-credits). The diffuser assets include product-label artwork that the team has permission to distribute with the examples.
